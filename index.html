Shorts Assistant

Game highlights for FC Online

Shorts Assistant is a desktop application (Windows) that turns FC Online gameplay recordings into short vertical highlight clips and lets the user upload those clips to their own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The user selects gameplay recordings stored on their computer.
  2. The app detects highlights and renders short vertical clips locally.
  3. The user reviews each clip, writes a caption, picks privacy and interaction settings, and connects their TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  4. The app uploads the chosen clip with the TikTok Content Posting API, either as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a direct post, depending on what the user picks.
