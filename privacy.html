Privacy Policy

Shorts Assistant · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary

Shorts Assistant ("the App") runs on your computer. It has no server of its own and sends no data to us. The only outside service it talks to is TikTok, and only so it can do what you ask.

Data the App uses

  • Your videos. Gameplay recordings and clips you select. They are processed on your computer and uploaded to TikTok only when you start or schedule an upload.
  • TikTok account information (scope user.info.basic): open ID, display name, and avatar, so the App can show which account is connected.
  • TikTok access tokens. Stored in the operating system's secure credential store (Windows Credential Manager). They are used only to call TikTok's API for you.
  • Posting data (scopes video.upload, video.publish): the caption, privacy level, and interaction settings you choose for each post, plus the upload status TikTok returns.
  • Video statistics (scope video.list): view, like, comment, and share counts for your public TikTok videos, so the App can show how your posts perform.

Where the data is stored

Everything is kept locally on your computer: in a local database in the App's data folder, and in the operating system's credential store for tokens. We do not run servers, use analytics, or show ads, and we never sell or share your data.

Third parties

When you connect your account or upload a video, the App sends data straight to TikTok (open.tiktokapis.com). TikTok's handling of that data is covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting an account in the App revokes the App's access at TikTok and deletes the account's tokens and local record from your computer.
  • You can also revoke the App's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions.
  • Uninstalling the App and deleting its data folder removes all local data.

Children

The App is not meant for anyone under the minimum age TikTok requires to have an account.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page with its "last updated" date.
